              🧮 Clinical Calculators

              eGFR — CKD-EPI 2021

              Estimates glomerular filtration rate using the 2021 CKD-EPI equation (race-free). Use for staging CKD and guiding prescribing decisions.

              CKD-EPI 2021 (Inker et al., NEJM 2021). Does not include race variable. Creatinine assay assumed to be IDMS-traceable.

              Creatinine Clearance — Cockcroft-Gault

              Estimates creatinine clearance for drug dosing. Most medicine datasheets reference this formula — use for dose adjustment in renal impairment.

              Cockcroft DW, Gault MH. Nephron. 1976. Use actual body weight unless BMI > 30, in which case use ideal body weight (calculate with the IBW tab).

              Body Mass Index

              Calculates BMI from weight and height with NHS weight category interpretation. Note: BMI is a screening tool and does not directly measure body fat.

              NHS weight categories for adults. For patients of Asian background, lower thresholds may apply (overweight ≥ 23, obese ≥ 27.5).

              Ideal & Adjusted Body Weight

              Devine formula IBW and adjusted body weight. Use IBW (or ABW if obese) for dosing renally-cleared or narrow-therapeutic-index medicines.

              Devine BJ. Drug Intell Clin Pharm. 1974. ABW = IBW + 0.4 × (actual weight − IBW). ABW is only applicable when actual weight exceeds IBW.

              HbA1c Converter

              Converts between IFCC (mmol/mol) and NGSP (%) units. UK labs report in mmol/mol; some clinical guidelines and older references use %.

              IFCC–NGSP master equation. NICE type 2 diabetes diagnosis threshold: ≥ 48 mmol/mol (6.5%). HbA1c target in type 2 diabetes (on medication): typically 48–58 mmol/mol (6.5–7.5%).

              Alcohol Unit Calculator

              Calculate units for patient counselling, AUDIT scoring and medicines interactions. UK low-risk guidelines: ≤ 14 units/week for both men and women.

              1 UK unit = 10 mL (8 g) pure alcohol. Formula: units = (volume mL × ABV%) ÷ 1000. UK Chief Medical Officers' low-risk guidelines: no more than 14 units per week, spread over 3+ days.

              Blood Pressure — 7-Day Home Monitor Log

              Enter readings taken at home over 7 days (morning and evening). Per NICE NG136, Day 1 readings are excluded from the average. Averaged results are interpreted against NICE HBPM thresholds.

              📋 NICE NG136 protocol Take 2 readings 1 min apart, morning & evening, for 7 days. Sit quietly for 5 mins before measuring. Discard Day 1 — average Days 2–7.

              Staff Statement & Reflective Account
              Purpose of this form
              This form gives you the chance to set out, in your own words, your involvement in a significant event, clinical or prescribing error, or near-miss. The aim is learning and improvement, not blame — please be as open and honest as you can.
              Section 1 – Incident details
              Section 2 – Your details
              Section 3 – Factual account of your involvement

              Please describe factually, in your own words, what part you played. Stick to what you did, saw and know; it is fine to say if you are unsure or cannot remember something.

              For example: what you did, on which day, and at what stage of the process.

              Include timings if you can, who else was involved, and anything that was handed over to or from you.

              For example: workload and time pressure, staffing, clinical system access or IT issues (e.g. SystmOne/EMIS), interruptions, anything unusual about the day.

              Section 4 – Your reflection

              This section is about your own thoughts and learning. Be honest — showing insight into what happened is exactly what is wanted here.

              Think about the steps, systems or checks that did not work as intended, and why.

              There is no wrong answer — this helps us understand and support you.

              About the task, the checks, working under pressure, asking for help, or handing work over.

              Concrete changes to how you will work.

              Your view on current or proposed changes, and any further suggestions.

              Section 5 – Support, training or resources

              For example: refresher training, supervision, clearer procedures, workload or rota changes.

              Section 6 – Declaration & signature

              I confirm that this statement and reflection are true and accurate to the best of my knowledge and belief, and are written in my own words.
